Pauses and speech are found from the recording itself. The floor is 6 dB above the quietest tenth of the 16 ms blocks. A pause block lies at most 1 dB above the floor, a speech block more than 10 dB above it. Noise reduction is the energy of the pauses before divided by after. Speech level change compares the energy of the speech blocks, which still include the noise under the speech, so a small negative value is expected.
